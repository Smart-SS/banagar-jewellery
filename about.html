The Man Behind the Craft

A Goldsmith Who Bankers Trust

Anil is a master goldsmith with over 25 years of hands-on experience crafting and repairing fine gold jewellery. Based in Hyderabad, he has built a reputation for unmatched precision, honest pricing, and exceptional artistry.

Beyond his workshop, Anil serves as an official Certified Gold Appraiser for Bank of India — one of the most trusted roles in the precious metals industry. His valuations are used by the bank to assess gold offered as collateral for loans, making his word as good as gold — literally.

Whether you need a bridal jewellery set crafted from scratch, an heirloom repaired with care, or a certified valuation certificate for your bank, Anil brings the same standard of integrity and expertise to every job.
